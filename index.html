Deflectometry Learning Lab

Learn the method, design a bench, replicate a published experiment.

Measure the shape of a mirror with a screen and a camera

Deflectometry displays a known pattern on a screen and photographs its reflection in the surface under test. Every change in the surface slope moves the reflected pattern; decoding that movement gives the slope at every point, and integrating the slopes gives the shape.

This lab is organised in three tracks. Learn builds the concepts for any deflectometry method. Design sizes a real bench from one shared set of values. Replicate follows a published experiment from the paper to working code.

Which path?

  • New to deflectometry: follow Learn in order; each module ends with questions to check understanding.
  • Designing a bench: load a preset on D0, then go D1 → D5. Each page uses only values fixed on the pages before it.
  • Reproducing a paper: start at R1. The Choi et al. preset on D0 loads the paper's own geometry.

One shared bench

The Design pages share one set of values — object, screen, camera, geometry — stored in your browser. Change the gap on one page and every other page uses the new gap. Presets load complete benches (the ASU bench, the Choi et al. 2017 experiment, a single flat mirror); Export saves your bench as a file to hand in or share.
