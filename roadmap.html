Why the hardware can wait for the abstract

  • The Dec 10 deadline is for a poster abstract (≥ 250 words). The poster itself is presented on 8–11 Mar 2027.
  • E1 (repeatability) needs no actuator: nothing is moved during the 30 captures.
  • A manual tilt with the laser reference (C7.1) shows tilt detection before the MPIA10 arrive.
  • E2 (piezo linearity) and E3 (drift) move to January and go on the poster.
