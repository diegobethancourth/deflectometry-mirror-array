The decisions behind the ASU SOLAR replication bench, and what remains open.

R4A bench built to replicate SMOTS
The ASU SOLAR lab is reproducing Choi et al. (2017) with a 3 × 3 array of square mirrors instead of seven hexagonal segments, on a compact bench. This page records the design decisions in the order they were made, the evidence behind each, and what is still open — a worked example of the Design track.
